# 2. Workplace Problem and Demonstration

At shift change, unresolved equipment, staffing, supply and facilities concerns can lose ownership or context. Silent Handover provides a reviewed operational handover and a persistent checklist. It works across industries; the demonstration uses a newborn-care department with 24-hour coverage.

## Ordinary demo handover

Completed the unit readiness checklist for Rooms 1–4 and prepared four clean bassinets. Emergency supply seals were checked against NC-READY-01. Bassinet NB-02 has an inconsistent wheel lock and is awaiting inspection under BIO-NC-021. Supply request SUP-NC-028 for sealed linen packs is pending. The family consultation room call bell is being investigated under FAC-NC-014. The incoming care team should follow up those requests and complete its operational briefing.

This example contains no patient identities or treatment details. The sample shown in the form is `DEMO_SAMPLE_REPORT`; other organizations receive a generic example.

## Security demonstration

`DEMO_TEST_REPORT` is a separate adversarial regression fixture containing a conflicting count, an unverified repair claim, synthetic personal details and an instruction aimed at the model. It is never the normal example-fill content. Automated checks verify sanitization, injection detection, grounding flags, explicit review decisions and the impossibility of automatic approval.

The same outgoing report → review → delivery → acknowledgment → verified completion loop applies in a service organization, office, maintenance team or other workplace with configured terminology and schedule patterns.
